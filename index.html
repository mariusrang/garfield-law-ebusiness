§1

The pricing evidence

Garfield's prices for undisputed steps match, pound for pound, the fixed costs that Practice Direction 45 lets a creditor recover from the debtor. On an undisputed claim that is eventually paid, the creditor's net legal cost falls to £9.50.

§4

AI-native law firms, 2024–2026

Garfield's own milestones are marked in colour.

  1. Jan 2025
    Crosby launches in the US: contract review by an AI-powered law firm, backed by Sequoia.
  2. 14 Mar 2025
    Garfield.Law Limited becomes an SRA-licensed body; it had been incorporated in May 2023 as Project Garfield Ltd.
  3. 6 May 2025
    The SRA announces Garfield as the first purely AI-based firm authorised in England & Wales.
  4. Aug 2025
    More than a dozen law firms use Garfield under their own brand; three-fifths of users are not lawyers.
  5. Sep 2025
    Lawhive, an AI legal platform, buys the regulated firm Woodstock Legal Services.
  6. Nov 2025
    Norm Ai launches Norm Law, an AI-native firm for financial institutions, backed by Blackstone.
  7. 2025
    Counter-example: the US FTC orders DoNotPay, a “robot lawyer” that was not a law firm, to pay $193,000 over unsupported claims.
  8. 14 May 2026
    Garfield wins its first contested trial: £7,000 recovered for “around £400” in its fees, plus an undisclosed barrister fee. 600+ claims, £500k+ “recovered or resolved”.
