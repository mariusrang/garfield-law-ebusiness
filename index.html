Exhibit 3

AI-native law firms, 2024–2026

Garfield is part of a wider move: firms that own both the AI and the regulated law firm, and do not bill by the hour. Garfield milestones are marked in orange.

  1. Dec 2024
    Lawhive raises a $40m Series A for its fixed-fee legal platform with AI assistant "Lawrence".
  2. Jan 2025
    Crosby soft-launches in the US: an AI-powered law firm for contract review, seed-funded by Sequoia.
  3. Mar 2025
    Garfield.Law quietly starts taking its first users.
  4. 6 May 2025
    SRA announces Garfield as the first purely AI-based firm authorised in England & Wales.
  5. Jul–Aug 2025
    Garfield issues its first claim forms; 12+ law firms use it under their own brand.
  6. Sep 2025
    Lawhive buys Woodstock Legal Services: a tech platform acquires a regulated law firm.
  7. Nov 2025
    Norm Ai launches Norm Law, an AI-native firm for financial institutions, backed by Blackstone.
  8. Mar 2026
    Crosby raises a $60m Series B to "replace" the billable hour with outcome-based billing.
  9. 14 May 2026
    Garfield wins its first contested trial: £7,000 recovered for about £400 in fees. 600+ claims started, £500k+ recovered.
